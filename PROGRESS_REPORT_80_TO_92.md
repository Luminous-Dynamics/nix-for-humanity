## 🔑 Lessons Learned

### What Worked
1. **Fix the worst first**: 0% dev queries were top priority
2. **Patterns before ML**: Specialists give instant results
3. **Data quality matters**: 566 good examples > 10K bad ones
4. **Rapid iteration**: Ship improvements immediately

### What Didn't Work
1. **Pure neural approach**: Couldn't handle all cases
2. **Complex architectures**: Simple LSTM sufficient
3. **Perfectionism**: 92% shipped > 95% planned
